The Book

This book is created for children aged 8 to 10 years old, a stage when learners are curious about the world and beginning to develop logical thinking and visual understanding.

It helps children explore how artificial intelligence (AI) sees, thinks, and learns in ways that are different from humans. It also encourages children to think critically about technology while understanding the partnership between humans and AI.

All illustrations in this book were created with the help of Nano Banana AI. Special thanks for supporting the visual storytelling of this project.
