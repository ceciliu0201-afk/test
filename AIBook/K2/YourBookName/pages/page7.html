The Book

This book is created for children aged 8 to 10 years old, a stage when learners are curious about the world and beginning to develop logical thinking and visual understanding. Through engaging stories and colorful illustrations, the book introduces technology concepts in a way that is easy and enjoyable for young readers. The core idea of the book is to help children understand how artificial intelligence (AI) sees, thinks, and learns differently from humans. While people understand meaning, emotions, and real-life experiences, AI relies on visual data, pattern recognition, and training from humans to make decisions. The book shows that AI does not “think” like a person, but learns by recognizing examples and improving over time. By exploring the relationship between humans and AI, the book emphasizes that people play an essential role in guiding and teaching AI responsibly. Children are encouraged to reflect on how technology works and how humans can use it wisely, building early awareness of both AI knowledge and digital responsibility.
