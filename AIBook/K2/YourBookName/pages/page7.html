The Book

This book is created for children aged 8 to 10 years old, a stage when learners are curious about the world and beginning to develop logical thinking and visual understanding. The book introduces technology concepts in a way that is easy and enjoyable for young readers.

By exploring the relationship between humans and AI, the book emphasizes that people play an essential role in guiding and teaching AI responsibly. Children are encouraged to reflect on how technology works and how humans can use it wisely, building early awareness of both AI knowledge and digital responsibility.
